## Boopsign vs PandaDoc: Decision Factors That Matter

### Signer Friction and Completion Rate

If a signer faces account prompts or multiple confirmation steps, completion drops. Low-friction flows consistently convert faster.

### Time to Send and Operational Speed

Fast preparation keeps momentum after sales calls and proposal approvals. Slower tools often increase follow-up workload.

### Mobile Signing Quality

Many recipients sign on mobile. A touch-first interface reduces errors and abandonment.

### Pricing Clarity at Real Usage Levels

Compare monthly spend based on your actual document volume, including add-ons and seat costs.

### Legal Records and Audit Quality
