Google Docs is the go-to tool for collaboration and drafting. However, when it comes to the final step—signing the document—many people realize that Google Docs doesn't have a secure, built-in e-signature tool that provides a legal audit trail.

Simply typing your name or inserting an image of your signature into a Google Doc isn't enough for high-stakes business agreements. In this guide, we’ll show you the correct way to sign a Google Doc.

## Why You Shouldn't "Sign" Inside Google Docs
-   **No Tamper-Evidence:** Anyone with access to the document can delete or move your signature image.
-   **No Audit Trail:** Google Docs tracks *edits*, but it doesn't track *legal intent to sign* with IP addresses and timestamps.
-   **Format Shifts:** If you send a live Google Doc for signature, the other person might accidentally (or intentionally) change the text of the agreement before signing.

## The Solution: The "Freeze and Sign" Workflow

### Step 1: Finalize Your Draft
Ensure all your comments are resolved and your formatting is correct. Once the document is ready, go to:
-   **File** > **Download** > **PDF Document (.pdf)**

Converting to PDF "freezes" the content, ensuring that no one can change the terms after you’ve applied your signature.

### Step 2: Upload to Boopsign
Head over to [Boopsign](/) and drag your newly downloaded PDF into the upload area. 

### Step 3: Add Your Signature
1.  **Select the Signature Tool:** Choose whether you want to type, draw, or upload your signature.
2.  **Place the Fields:** If you need someone else to sign (like a client), add their email address and place a signature field for them as well.
3.  **Finish:** Once you apply your signature, Boopsign will cryptographically "seal" the document.

### Step 4: Share and Track
If you need others to sign, hit **Send**. They will receive a secure link to the frozen, professional PDF. You’ll be notified the moment they view it and the moment they sign it.

## Alternative: Using the Boopsign Google Workspace Add-on
If you use Google Docs frequently, you can simplify this process even further by using our official add-on.

1.  Open your Google Doc.
2.  Go to **Extensions** > **Add-ons** > **Get add-ons**.
3.  Search for **Boopsign** and install it.
4.  Now, you can send documents for signature directly from the Google Docs sidebar without ever leaving your browser tab.

| Traditional Way | The Boopsign Way |

## Conclusion
Don't let the simplicity of Google Docs lead to a security gap in your contracts. By moving your final signing process to Boopsign, you ensure your business remains protected and your documents remain professional.
