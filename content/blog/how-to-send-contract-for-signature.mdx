Sending a contract used to involve attachments, printing, and a lot of back-and-forth emails. Today, the most professional way to do it is by sending a secure web link. This not only makes it easier for your client but also gives you a verifiable audit trail.

Here is the step-by-step process to send your next contract using Boopsign.

## 1. Upload Your Document
Start by uploading your contract (PDF, Word, or Google Doc) to Boopsign. 
-   **Pro Tip:** If you frequently send the same contract (like a standard Service Agreement), save it as a **Template** first. This will save you from having to place signature fields every single time.

## 2. Add Your Recipients
Who needs to sign? Enter their name and email address. 
-   **Signing Order:** If you need to sign *after* your client has reviewed the document, you can set a "Signing Order." This ensures the document only comes to you once the client has finished.
-   **CC Recipients:** Need to keep your accountant or project manager in the loop? Add them as a CC so they receive a finalized copy automatically.

## 3. Prepare the Document (Drag & Drop)
This is where you tell your client exactly where to sign. Drag the following fields onto your document:
-   **Signature:** The main area where they will draw or type their name.
-   **Date:** This field can be set to "Auto-Fill," so the client doesn't even have to type the date.
-   **Text Fields:** Use these for job titles, company names, or addresses.
-   **Initials:** Great for long contracts where you want them to acknowledge specific pages.

## 4. Customize the Message
Don't just send a generic "Signature Requested" email. Use the "Email Message" area to add a personal note:
> *"Hi Sarah, it was great speaking today! Here is the SOW we discussed. Please take a look and sign when you're ready so we can kick off on Monday."*

## 5. Send and Track
Once you hit send, Boopsign takes over. You can track the status in real-time from your dashboard:
-   **Sent:** The email has landed in their inbox.
-   **Viewed:** The client has opened the link (great for knowing when to follow up).
-   **Signed:** The deal is done!

---

## What Happens Next?
As soon as the final person signs, Boopsign automatically:
1.  **Generates a "Sealed" PDF** that cannot be tampered with.
2.  **Emails a copy** to all parties involved.
3.  **Attaches an Audit Trail** with IP addresses and timestamps, ensuring the document is legally binding.

## Common Mistake to Avoid:
Don't send your contract as a raw email attachment (PDF or Word). This forces the client to find their own way to sign it, which often leads to "I'll do it later." By sending a Boopsign link, you remove all technical excuses for a delay.
