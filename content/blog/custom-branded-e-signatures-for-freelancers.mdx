## Is a Branded E-Signature Legally Binding?

Yes. Custom branding does not change the legal validity of the signature. Boopsign is fully compliant with the **ESIGN Act** and **UETA** in the United States, and **eIDAS** in the European Union.

![Signature Interface](/screenshots/sign.png)

Every signed document comes with a secure **Audit Trail** that includes:
- Timestamped entries for every action (Created, Viewed, Signed).
- IP addresses of all parties involved.
- Unique document IDs and checksums.
- A tamper-evident digital seal that breaks if the PDF is modified.
