At Boopsign, we believe that the ability to sign documents securely and legally shouldn't be gated behind a high monthly fee—especially when you're just starting your business. 

One of the most common questions we get is: **"Is Boopsign actually free?"**

The answer is **yes**, we offer a robust free tier that is perfect for freelancers, individuals, and small startups. Here is everything you need to know about our pricing and what you get for $0.

## The Free Plan: What’s Included?
Unlike some "free trials" that expire after 14 days, Boopsign's Free Plan is a permanent tier. It includes:

-   **3 Documents per Month:** Send up to three unique documents for signature every single month.
-   **Unlimited Signers:** Need four people to sign one contract? No problem. We don’t limit the number of signers per document.
-   **Mobile Signing:** Your clients can sign from any device without an account.
-   **Audit Trails:** Every document includes a basic certificate of completion with timestamps and IP logging.
-   **Security:** You get the same bank-level AES-256 encryption as our enterprise users.

## Is Signing Free for My Clients?
**Always.** Your clients never have to pay to sign a document you send them. They also don't have to create an account, install an app, or jump through any hoops. They just click, sign, and finish.

## When Should You Upgrade to Pro?
While our free plan is generous, our **Pro Plan ($15/month)** is designed for power users who need more volume and branding control. You should consider upgrading if you need:

### 1. Unlimited Documents
If you’re sending more than three contracts a month, the Pro plan gives you unlimited sending power. No "per-envelope" fees, ever.

### 2. Custom Branding (White Label)
Want the signing experience to look like your brand? The Pro plan allows you to add your company logo to emails and the signing interface, removing Boopsign’s default branding.

### 3. Advanced Templates
Save your most-used documents (like NDAs or Service Agreements) as templates. This allows you to send them for signature in two clicks instead of re-uploading every time.

### 4. Automatic Reminders
Save time by letting Boopsign automatically follow up with signers who haven't completed their documents.
