## 5 Best Practices for Modern Contract Management

### 1. Simplify the Language
Legal documents don't have to be unreadable. Use plain English wherever possible. If your contract is easy to understand, your clients will sign it faster. Avoid unnecessary "legalese" that can lead to confusion and delays.

### 2. Standardize Your Templates
Consistency is key. Instead of creating a new contract from scratch every time, use standardized templates for common agreements like NDAs, Service Agreements, and Statements of Work (SOW). This reduces errors and saves hours of work.

### 3. Use an Orderly Signing Sequence
If multiple people need to sign, set a specific order. For example, have your client sign first, and then have your internal team member counter-sign. This ensures that the agreement only becomes final once all preliminary reviews are complete.

### 4. Provide Context
When you send a contract through Boopsign, include a personalized message. Explain what the document is, why it's being sent now, and what the next steps are. A little communication goes a long way in building trust.

### 5. Review the Audit Trail
After a document is signed, don't just look at the signature. Review the audit trail to confirm the IP address, timestamp, and verification method. This data is your primary defense if a signature is ever challenged.

## The Recommended Workflow

A repeatable flow teams rely on to keep contracts consistent and defensible.

1.  **Prepare:** Start with an approved template, fill in the specific variables, and attach any necessary exhibits or appendices.
2.  **Invite:** Send the invitation via Boopsign. Use professional branding so the signer recognizes your company immediately.
3.  **Monitor:** Track the status of the document. If it hasn't been opened after 48 hours, send a gentle manual reminder or let Boopsign's auto-reminders do the work.
4.  **Execute:** Once all parties have signed, all participants should automatically receive a fully executed copy with the certificate of completion.
