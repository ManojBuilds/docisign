## 2. Adobe Acrobat Sign
If you’re already paying for the full Adobe Creative Cloud suite, Adobe Sign might seem like the logical choice.

-   **The Good:** Deep integration with Photoshop and Acrobat.
-   **The Bad:** The interface is notoriously clunky, and the mobile experience often forces clients to download an app or navigate a complex portal.

## 3. DocuSign (The "Safe" Corporate Choice)
DocuSign is the most recognized name in the industry. Big corporate clients often specifically request it because it's what their legal team uses.

-   **The Good:** Universal recognition.
-   **The Bad:** It's incredibly expensive for solo pros, and the "forced login" flow for signers often causes 2–3 days of unnecessary delay.

## How to Choose Your Tool
When selecting an app, ask yourself these three questions:

1.  **Does it require my client to create an account?** (If yes, expect significant delays).
2.  **Does it look like it belongs in 2026?** (Aesthetics matter for creative businesses).
3.  **Are there hidden fees?** (Enterprise tools often charge "envelope" fees after a certain volume).
