You’ve done the hard work. You’ve had the discovery calls, sent the proposal, and the client said, "This looks great, send over the contract!" 

But then... silence. Two days pass. Then a week. Suddenly, your project start date is at risk, and you're starting to wonder if the deal is falling through.

Why do clients ghost when it comes to signing? It’s rarely because they've changed their minds about working with you. Usually, it's one of these five friction points. Here’s how to fix them.

## 1. The "Scanner Struggle"
If you are still sending a PDF and asking your client to "print, sign, and scan," you are effectively giving them a chore. 

**The Fix:** Use a mobile-first e-signature tool like Boopsign. When a client can sign with a single tap on their phone while waiting for coffee, they’ll do it immediately. If they have to wait until they are back at their desk (and their printer actually works), you’re adding days of delay.

## 2. Decision Paralysis (Legal Overload)
If your "standard agreement" is 15 pages of dense legalese for a $2,000 project, you're scaring your client. They feel like they need to send it to a lawyer, and lawyers are expensive and slow.

**The Fix:** Strip your contract down to the essentials.
- What are you doing? 
- How much are you being paid? 
- When is it due? 
- Who owns the work?
- How do we end the relationship?

If you can get it down to 2-3 pages of plain English, signing becomes a "micro-decision" rather than a legal event.

## 3. Fear of Commitment (The "Exit" Anxiety)
Sometimes clients hesitate because they feel "locked in." If they don't see a clear way to cancel the project if things go south, they’ll wait until they are 110% sure—which might be never.

**The Fix:** Include a clear, fair "Termination" clause right at the top. Something like: *"Either party can end this project with 7 days' notice. If ended, the client pays for all work completed up to that date."* This safety net actually makes it easier for them to say "Yes" today.

## 4. Account Requirement Friction
Many enterprise e-signature tools force signers to create an account, verify their email, and set a password just to sign a document. 

**The Fix:** Use a "No-Account-Required" platform. Boopsign allows your clients to view and sign your documents directly from a secure link. Zero friction = 100% higher completion rates.

## 5. The "I'll Do It Later" Trap
Most people live in their inboxes. Your contract email gets buried under 50 other messages. They mean to sign it, but they simply forget.

**The Fix:** Automated Reminders. Don't be the person manually emailing "Hey, checking in on that contract?" every two days. It feels desperate. Instead, let Boopsign send a professional "Your signature is requested" reminder every 48 hours automatically.
