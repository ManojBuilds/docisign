Security isn't just a checkbox for us—it's the foundation of everything we build. When you handle sensitive contracts, legal agreements, or personal documents, you need to know that your data is protected by industry-leading standards.

In this guide, we'll walk you through the multi-layered security approach Boopsign takes to ensure your documents remain private, secure, and legally binding.

## Encryption: Protecting Data at Every Stage

We use industry-standard encryption and modern key management to keep documents private and tamper-evident. Protection happens in three main stages:

### 1. Data in Transit
All network traffic between your device and our servers is protected using **TLS 1.3 (Transport Layer Security)**. This ensures that any data sent—whether you're uploading a contract or signing a document—is encrypted and shielded from interception.

### 2. Data at Rest
Once your documents are stored on our servers, they are encrypted using **AES-256 (Advanced Encryption Standard)**. We use Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) to manage encryption keys, ensuring that even if physical storage were compromised, your data remains unreadable.

### 3. Tamper-Evident Audit Trails
Every document signed through Boopsign includes a cryptographic hash. This means that if even a single pixel or character is changed after the document is finalized, the audit trail will immediately flag the document as invalid.

## Infrastructure Security

Our platform is hosted on Tier-1 cloud providers (AWS/Google Cloud) that maintain strict physical and environmental security controls.

- **ISO 27001 & SOC 2:** Our infrastructure providers undergo regular third-party audits to maintain these gold-standard security certifications.
- **Regular Penetration Testing:** We conduct routine vulnerability scans and third-party security audits to identify and fix potential threats before they can be exploited.
- **Redundant Storage:** Your documents are backed up across multiple geographic zones to ensure 99.99% availability and data integrity.

## User Access Controls

Security is also about making sure only the right people have access to your documents.

1. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Protect your account with an extra layer of security.
2. **Access Logs:** See exactly who viewed, downloaded, or signed a document and when.
3. **Role-Based Permissions:** For teams, you can control exactly who has permission to create, send, or view specific templates.
