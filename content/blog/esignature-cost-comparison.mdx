## Why the Massive Price Gap?

Enterprise tools aren't expensive because they sign documents "better." At its core, the technology behind a digital signature is a standard cryptographic process.

The high cost of enterprise tools covers:
-   **Sales Commissions:** High-touch sales teams that target Fortune 500 companies.
-   **Support Bloat:** Maintaining massive support infrastructures for thousands of employees.
-   **Feature Overkill:** Building complex integration tools (like SAP or Oracle connectors) that 99% of small businesses will never use.

If you are a team of 1-10 people, you are essentially subsidizing the massive marketing budgets of the incumbents.

## Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When comparing prices, the headline number usually doesn't tell the whole story. Look out for these "gotchas" in larger platforms:

1.  **Envelope/Document Limits:** Many "Basic" plans (which still cost $15-$25/mo) limit you to just 5 or 10 documents per month. If you send more, your bill can double or triple instantly.
2.  **Branding Fees:** Want to put your logo on the emails your clients see? Many competitors gate this behind their most expensive "Pro" or "Business" tiers.
3.  **The "Signer Tax":** Some platforms make it difficult for your clients to sign without seeing ads for the tool or being forced to create their own accounts. This adds friction to your deals.
