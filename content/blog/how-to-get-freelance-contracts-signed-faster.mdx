## The Signature Speed Imperative

Every day a contract remains unsigned is a day you're not getting paid. More importantly, delayed signatures often correlate with project delays, scope creep, and client dissatisfaction. Speed isn't just about convenience—it's about maintaining momentum and securing your business.

## The 3-Minute Signature Framework

The fastest way to get contracts signed combines three elements:

1. **Eliminate Friction:** Remove barriers to signing
2. **Optimize for Mobile:** Meet clients where they are
3. **Follow Up Strategically:** Stay top-of-mind without being pushy

## Strategy 1: Eliminate Signature Friction

### Skip the Account Creation

The #1 reason contracts go unsigned? Account creation requirements. When you send a contract that requires clients to create an account, set a password, and verify their email, you're asking them to invest 10-15 minutes in a process they see as administrative overhead.

- **Large tap targets:** Make signature areas at least 44px x 44px

- **Auto-save:** Prevent loss of progress if they navigate away

## Tools and Techniques for Faster Signatures

### 1. Pre-Fill What You Know

If you know your client's name, company, or address, fill it in for them. Every field they don't have to complete is a speed boost.

### 2. Use Smart Defaults

Set common options as defaults (like payment terms or project length) so clients only need to change what's different.

### 3. Provide Context

Include a brief note explaining why the contract is important and what happens next. This creates urgency without pressure.

### 4. Set Expiration Dates

Contracts that never expire often get forgotten. A 14-day expiration creates gentle urgency.
