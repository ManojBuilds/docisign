"I sent the contract last week, but I'm still waiting for the signature."

If you’ve said this recently, you're not alone. But the reality is that the silence isn't usually about the *terms* of the contract—it's about the *friction* of the signing process. Every extra click, login, or software requirement gives your client a reason to close the tab and "deal with it later."

In this guide, we’ll show you how to eliminate that friction and get your contracts turned around in less than 3 minutes.

## 1. Eliminate the "Account Requirement" Barrier
The #1 killer of signature momentum is the mandatory account. If your client clicks your link and is greeted by a "Create an Account" or "Verify your Email" screen, they will likely bounce.

By using **Boopsign**, you provide a no-friction experience. Your clients click the link, review the document, and sign. No logins, no passwords, no obstacle.

## 2. Optimize for Mobile-First Signatures
More than 65% of business professionals now review and sign documents on their smartphones. If your contract arrives as a raw Word doc or a non-mobile-friendly PDF link, it will sit in their inbox until they reach a desktop.

## 3. The "Pre-Fill" Strategy
Don't ask your clients to do work you’ve already done. If you know their address, company name, or tax ID, pre-fill those fields for them. The less typing your client has to do, the faster they will finish.

## 4. Set Strategic Expiration Dates
A contract that never expires is a contract that never gets prioritized. Set a clear, gentle expiration date (typically 7–14 days). This creates a psychological "deadline" that moves your document from the "someday" pile to the "now" pile.

## 5. Sell the "Next Step," Not the "Legal Paperwork"
When you send the signature request, don't focus on the "contract." Focus on what happens *after* they sign.

**BAD:** *"Please sign the contract so we can comply with my legal requirements."*
**GOOD:** *"As soon as you sign here, I can get my team started on your homepage concepts today."*
