Whether you need to sign an offer letter, a sales contract, or a simple NDA, creating an electronic signature is the most efficient way to get it done. No printers, no scanners, and no wasted paper.

In this guide, we’ll show you the three primary ways to create a professional signature using Boopsign's free tools.

## Method 1: Type Your Signature (The Fastest Way)
If you want a clean, legible, and professional-looking signature without the shaky lines of a mouse, typing is the best option.

1.  **Open the Boopsign Editor:** Upload your document or use our [Online Signature Maker](/tools/online-signature-maker).
2.  **Select "Type":** Enter your full name.
3.  **Choose a Style:** Boopsign offers several curated "handwriting" fonts. We’ve selected styles that range from formal cursive to modern minimal.
4.  **Place:** Once you've picked a style, simply click on the document where you want it to appear.

## Method 2: Draw Your Signature (The Most Personalized)
If you want your electronic signature to look exactly like your "wet-ink" signature, drawing it is the way to go.

-   **On Mobile:** This is where drawing shines. Use your finger or a stylus on your phone's touchscreen. It feels natural and produces the most accurate results.
-   **On Desktop:** Use your mouse or trackpad. While it can take a few tries to get it perfect, Boopsign allows you to "Clear" and redraw as many times as you need.

## Method 3: Upload an Image (The "Classic" Copy)
If you already have a perfect signature written on paper that you’ve scanned or photographed, you can use that.

1.  **Sign a white piece of paper** with a dark pen (black ink works best).
2.  **Take a clear photo** or scan of the signature.
3.  **Upload to Boopsign:** Use our "Upload" tool to select the image.
4.  **Auto-Process:** Boopsign will attempt to remove the white background, leaving you with a transparent PNG that looks perfectly embedded in your digital document.

## Which Method Should You Use?

| Need | Recommended Method |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Speed & Legibility** | **Type** |
| **Authenticity (Looks Like You)** | **Draw (on Mobile)** |
| **Highest Fidelity** | **Upload Image** |

## Is a Created Signature Legally Binding?
**Yes.** Under laws like the ESIGN Act and eIDAS, the *method* you use to create the signature (typing vs. drawing) matters less than the *intent* to sign. As long as you are using a secure platform like Boopsign that tracks the transaction and provides an audit trail, your signature is fully legal.

## Pro Tips for a Great Signature:
-   **Keep it Consistent:** Try to use the same method for all your business documents to maintain a professional standard.
-   **Use Black Ink:** If you are uploading an image, black ink provides the best contrast for background removal.
-   **Sign on Mobile:** For the best drawing experience, always open your Boopsign links on your smartphone.
