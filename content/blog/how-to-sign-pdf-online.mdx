Getting a PDF that needs a signature is a common occurrence, but the old method of printing, signing with a pen, and scanning it back in is a massive waste of time. 

Today, you can sign a PDF online in seconds. In this tutorial, we’ll show you how to use Boopsign to sign any document from any device—completely for free.

## Step 1: Upload Your PDF
First, head over to the [Boopsign home page](/) or our [Free PDF Signer tool](/tools/online-signature-maker). Drag and drop your PDF file into the upload area. 

Boopsign supports all standard PDF versions and ensures your document is encrypted the moment it touches our servers.

## Step 2: Create Your Signature
Once your document is loaded, you’ll have three options to create your signature:
1.  **Type:** Choose from several professional handwriting fonts. Just type your name, and we’ll handle the rest.
2.  **Draw:** Use your mouse, trackpad, or touch screen to draw your signature exactly how you want it. This is the most popular option for mobile users.
3.  **Upload:** If you already have a high-quality scan of your signature, you can upload the image directly.

## Step 3: Place and Resize
Click on your signature to drop it onto the PDF. You can then:
-   **Drag** it to the correct signature line.
-   **Resize** it by pulling the corners so it fits perfectly.
-   **Add Extra Fields:** Often you need more than just a signature. You can also add text boxes for your printed name, today's date, or your job title.

## Step 4: Download Your Signed PDF
When you're happy with the placement, click "Finish" or "Download." Boopsign will process the document, apply a tamper-evident seal, and provide you with a high-quality PDF containing your signature.

## Why Use Boopsign instead of a basic PDF editor?

While some operating systems have "Preview" or "Markup" tools, Boopsign offers several critical advantages:

-   **Legally Binding:** We provide a digital audit trail that logs exactly when and where the document was signed.
-   **Mobile-Optimized:** Our interface is designed to work perfectly on smartphones, so you can sign on the go.
-   **No "Flattening" Issues:** Some editors create signatures that disappear when the file is opened in other apps. Boopsign signatures are permanently and securely embedded.
-   **Zero Cost:** You can sign and download your documents without ever reaching for your credit card.
