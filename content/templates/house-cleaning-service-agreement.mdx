# Free House Cleaning Service Agreement Template (2026) | Edit & Sign

Protect your cleaning business with a professional service agreement. Defines scope of work, access policies, liability limits, and cancellations.

## What's inside this template?

Ambiguity causes refunds. Our agreement template clarifies exactly what is included (and excluded) in your standard clean.

### Key Features

- **Scope of Services**: Definitively list standard tasks vs. extra services to avoid scope creep.
- **Supplies & Equipment**: Prevent damage claims by specifying if you use the client's vacuum or your own supplies.
- **Access & Lockouts**: If you show up and the key isn't there, you still get paid.
- **Liability Limit**: Limits your liability for breakage to a specific amount or to replacement value only.

## Why Digital Contracts?

Leaving a paper contract on the counter is unprofessional. Sending a digital link before you even arrive sets the tone for a premium service.

- **Reduce Cancellations**: Clients take you seriously when they sign a formal agreement.
- **Recurring Service**: Set up the terms for weekly or bi-weekly visits once.
- **Easy on Mobile**: Clients can sign from their smartphone instantly.

## Common Questions

### Do I need a contract for residential cleaning?

Yes. Even for small jobs, a contract protects you from liability (e.g., broken items) and ensures you get paid for last-minute cancellations.

### Who provides the vacuum and supplies?

Our template includes a 'Supplies' clause where you can specify if the client provides the vacuum/liquids or if you bring your own pro-grade equipment.

### Can I charge a lockout fee?

Yes. The 'Access & Lockout' clause allows you to charge a fee (e.g., 50% of the service) if you arrive and cannot get inside the home.

### Is this suitable for commercial cleaning?

This template is optimized for residential homes. For offices, we recommend editing the 'Scope of Work'.
