# Freelance Photographer Contract Template

Protect your photography business with usage rights, delivery timelines, and model releases. This contract protects your work, guarantees payment, and prevents scope creep.

## What's inside this contract?

A photographer-specific agreement that covers all the essentials without overwhelming legal jargon.

### Key Features

- **Clear Scope**: Defines exactly what you're delivering
- **Payment Protection**: Payment schedule with late fee clauses
- **IP Rights**: Client gets the work, you keep portfolio rights
- **Revision Limits**: Prevents endless revision requests

## Pro Tip for Photographers

Include a clause about weather/rescheduling for outdoor shoots, and always specify how many final edited photos you'll deliver. 'All photos' is a recipe for disaster.
