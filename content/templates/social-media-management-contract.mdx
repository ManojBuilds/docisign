# Free Social Media Management Contract Template (2026) | Edit & Sign

Stop scope creep and get paid on time. A professional contract template designed for SMM agencies and freelancers. Includes scope definitions and platform independence clauses.

## What's inside this template?

Social media management is prone to 'scope creep'—clients asking for just one more post or expecting 24/7 community management. Our template protects you with specific clauses designed for SMM agencies and freelancers.

### Key Features

- **Scope of Work & Deliverables**: Clearly define platform accounts (IG, LinkedIn), number of posts per week, and community management hours to prevent burnout.
- **Platform Independence**: Crucial clause that protects you if a platform (e.g., TikTok) changes its algorithm or goes down. You can't control the Zuck!
- **Content Approval Workflow**: Sets a deadline for clients to approve content (e.g., 48 hours). If they don't reply, it assumes approval to keep your schedule consistent.
- **IP & Access Rights**: Defines who owns the content created and ensures you have legal access to their accounts without liability for previous posts.
