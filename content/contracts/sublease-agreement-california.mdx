## Common Questions

### Is subletting legal in California?

Yes, but only with landlord permission. California Civil Code Section 1995.230 requires written consent from your landlord before subletting. This template includes landlord approval language.

### Am I still liable if my subtenant doesn't pay?

Yes. You remain fully responsible to your landlord for rent and damages. That's why security deposits and thorough tenant screening are critical.

### Can my landlord refuse to let me sublease?

Yes, unless your lease explicitly allows subletting. Most California leases require landlord approval, which cannot be unreasonably withheld.

### What's the difference between subletting and assigning a lease?

Subletting means you stay on the lease and remain liable. Assignment transfers the lease entirely. This is a sublet agreement where you stay responsible.
