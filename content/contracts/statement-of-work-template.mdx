Stop scope creep before it starts. This SOW template helps you document exactly what you're building, when you're delivering it, and how it gets approved.

## What's inside this SOW?

A comprehensive project definition document that prevents misunderstandings and protects you from endless revision requests.

### Key Features

- **Clear Deliverables**: List exactly what you're delivering and when
- **Milestone Tracking**: Break projects into manageable phases with dates
- **Acceptance Criteria**: Define how deliverables get approved (critical!)
- **Out of Scope Section**: Explicitly state what's NOT included

## Pro Tip for Freelancers

The "Out of Scope" section is your best friend. List every common request that clients might assume is included but isn't. This saves hours of awkward conversations later.

- **Prevents Scope Creep**: Clear boundaries from day one
- **Mobile Signing**: Clients approve SOWs on their phone
- **Track Changes**: Document all scope modifications
