Stop scope creep and get paid on time. A professional contract template designed for freelancers. Includes scope definitions and IP protection clauses.

## What's inside this template?

Freelancing is prone to scope creep—clients asking for just one more revision or expecting unlimited revisions. Our template protects you with specific clauses designed for freelancers.

### Key Features

- **Scope of Work & Deliverables**: Clearly define deliverables, number of revisions, and timeline to prevent scope creep.
- **Payment Terms & Late Fees**: Protect yourself with clear payment schedules and penalties for late payments.
- **IP & Ownership Rights**: Defines who owns the final work and ensures you retain rights to your process work.
- **Cancellation Policy**: Protects both parties if either needs to terminate the agreement early.

## Why use Boopsign for this contract?

Most clients read contracts on their phone while between meetings. Sending a Word doc or PDF they have to print/scan is a deal-killer.

- **3-Minute Signing**: Clients click one link and sign with their finger.

## Common Questions

### Is this freelance contract template legally binding?

Yes, once filled out and signed by both parties, this contract constitutes a legally binding agreement. Electronic signatures added via Boopsign especially are fully compliant with the ESIGN Act and UETA.

### Do I need a lawyer to use this template?

While we always recommend consulting a lawyer for complex situations, this standard template covers the essential protections most freelancers need to get started safely.

### Can I edit the clauses in this template?

Absolutely. The template is designed to be customizable. You can add specific deliverables, change payment terms, or include unique clauses directly in the editor.

### Does the client need an account to sign?

No. With Boopsign, your client receives a secure link and can sign the contract on their phone or computer in seconds without creating an account or downloading an app.
