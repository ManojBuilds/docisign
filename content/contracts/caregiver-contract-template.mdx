## What's in this caregiver contract?

A comprehensive agreement that protects your loved one, your family, and the caregiver. Covers duties, schedule, emergency procedures, and legal requirements.

### Key Features

- **Duties Defined**: Clear breakdown of personal care, medical, and household tasks
- **Medical Information**: Document medications, allergies, and emergency contacts
- **Schedule & Pay**: Hourly rates, overtime, and payment schedule clearly stated
- **Confidentiality**: HIPAA-aligned privacy protections for medical information

## Why families need caregiver contracts

A verbal agreement falls apart when disputes arise about duties, hours, or pay. This contract protects everyone and ensures quality care for your loved one.

- **Legal Protection**: Clear terms prevent disputes and litigation
- **Quality Care**: Defined duties ensure consistent service
- **Emergency Preparedness**: Medical info and contacts documented

## Common Questions

### Do I need a contract for a family caregiver?

Yes, even for family members. A contract protects everyone by clarifying expectations, payment, duties, and legal requirements like tax withholding.

### What about taxes and insurance?

If you pay a caregiver more than $2,600/year (2024 threshold), you're likely a household employer and must withhold taxes. Consult a tax professional. Also consider workers' comp insurance.

### Should I do background checks?

Absolutely. For anyone providing care in your home, especially for vulnerable adults or children, run a criminal background check and verify references thoroughly.

### What if the caregiver needs to give medication?

Check your state laws—many states require training or certification for medication administration. Document all medications, dosages, and schedules clearly in the contract.
