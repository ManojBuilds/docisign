## Common Questions

### What should be in a digital marketing proposal?

A winning proposal should include an executive summary, client objectives, your proposed strategy (SEO, PPC, etc.), a detailed scope of work, project timeline, budget, and key performance indicators (KPIs).

### How do I calculate the budget for a marketing proposal?

You should typically break the budget down into 'Set-up Fees' (one-time) and 'Monthly Management Fees' (recurring). Be transparent about ad spend versus agency fees.

### Should I include case studies in the proposal?

Absolutely. Case studies or 'Social Proof' sections are essential for building trust. They show the client that you have successfully delivered results for similar businesses in the past.

### How do I handle revisions to the proposal?

Include a 'Validity Period' (e.g., 30 days) and a 'Scope Change' clause. This ensures that if the client asks for more work later, you have a process for adjusting the price.
