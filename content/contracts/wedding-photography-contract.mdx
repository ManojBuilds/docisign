# Free Wedding Photography Contract Template (2026) | Edit & Sign

Secure your dates and protect your images with a professional agreement designed for modern wedding photographers. Includes model releases, meal clauses, and cancellation policies.

## What's inside this template?

Wedding photography is high-stakes. Our template includes specific protections that generic contracts miss, ensuring you get fed, paid, and respected.

### Key Features

- **Model Release**: Grants you the explicit right to use the wedding photos for your portfolio, Instagram, and website marketing.
- **Retainer & Cancellation**: Protects your calendar. Clarifies that the booking fee is non-refundable if the client cancels specifically for this date.
- **Meals & Breaks**: It's a long day. This clause ensures you and your second shooter are provided a hot meal at the reception.
- **Harassment Policy**: Empowers you to stop coverage or leave if you or your staff are threatened or sexually harassed by guests.

## Why Speed Matters

Couples inquire with 5-10 photographers. The first one to get a signed contract often wins the date.

- **Send via Link**: Text the contract link directly to the bride or groom.
- **Mobile Optimized**: They can sign on their phone during their lunch break.
- **Instant Copy**: Everyone gets a PDF copy instantly upon signing.

## Common Questions

### Does this contract include a Model Release?

Yes, our template includes a standard Model Release clause that allows you to use the images for your portfolio, website, and social media marketing.

### What happens if the client cancels?

The template includes a Cancellation & Retainer clause. Typically, the retainer is non-refundable, and cancellations within 30 days of the wedding may require full payment. You can adjust these terms in the editor.

### Is there a 'Safe Working Environment' clause?

Absolutely. We include a harassment clause that protects you and your team from inappropriate behavior by guests, giving you the right to leave if the situation becomes unsafe.
