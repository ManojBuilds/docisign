Formalize your vendor relationships. This agreement protects your business from supply disruptions and ensures consistent product quality and pricing.

## What's inside this supply agreement?

A durable business contract designed to withstand the complexities of global and local supply chains.

### Key Features

- **Quality Assurance Specs**: Clearly defines the standards the goods must meet before they are accepted.
- **Delivery & Logistics Terms**: Outlines who pays for shipping, when ownership transfers (Incoterms), and what happens if a shipment is late.
- **Tiered Pricing Schedules**: Allows for volume discounts or seasonal price adjustments within a single document.
- **Indemnification Clauses**: Protects the buyer if the supplier's products cause injury or infringe on intellectual property.

## Why informal supply chains fail

One late delivery or a batch of bad parts can ruin your reputation. A formal agreement sets the 'rules of the road' for your vendors, ensuring they prioritize your needs.

- **Price Stability**: Locks in rates for a set period.
- **Reliability**: Defines strict delivery windows and penalties.
- **Scalability**: Makes it easy to increase orders as you grow.

## Common Questions

### What is the difference between a Supply Agreement and a Purchase Order?

A Supply Agreement is a long-term master contract that sets the general terms for all future interactions. A Purchase Order (PO) is a specific request for a single delivery under that master agreement.

### How do I handle price changes in a supply agreement?

You should include a 'Price Adjustment' clause. This can tie price changes to inflation, raw material costs, or require a 30-day notice period before any price increases take effect.

### What happens if the goods are defective?

The agreement includes a 'Warranty and Credit' section. This specifies how the buyer should notify the supplier of defects and whether the supplier will provide a refund, credit, or replacement.

### How can I terminate a supply agreement?

Most agreements allow for termination 'For Convenience' (with notice, e.g., 90 days) or 'For Cause' (if the other party breaches the contract, like failing to deliver on time multiple times).
