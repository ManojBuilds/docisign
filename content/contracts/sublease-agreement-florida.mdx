## Common Questions

### Is subleasing legal in Florida?

In Florida, subleasing is legal provided that it is not expressly forbidden in your master lease agreement. Most Florida leases require the landlord's prior written consent before a sublease can be executed.

### How long does a sublessor have to return a security deposit in Florida?

According to Florida Statute 83.49, you have 15 days to return the full deposit if no claim is being made. If you intend to make a claim for damages, you must notify the subtenant within 30 days.

### Do Florida subleases need to be notarized?

Generally, residential subleases in Florida do not need to be notarized to be legally binding. However, for leases longer than one year, notarization and witnesses are recommended for clarity and enforceability.

### Who is responsible for repairs in a Florida sublease?

Typically, the original landlord remains responsible for major structural repairs. The sublessor (you) is responsible for ensuring the property is habitable for the subtenant, and the subtenant is responsible for any damage they cause.
