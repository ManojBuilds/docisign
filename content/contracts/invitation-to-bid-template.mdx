Solicit professional bids for your project. This template ensures all contractors receive the same specifications for a fair and competitive bidding process.

## What's inside this invitation to bid?

A comprehensive solicitation document that covers all the critical details needed for a successful bidding cycle.

### Key Features

- **Detailed Scope of Work**: A section dedicated to exactly what needs to be done, leaving no room for assumptions.
- **Bid Form & Pricing Schedule**: A standardized table for contractors to enter their costs, ensuring easy comparison.
- **Project Milestones & Timeline**: Clearly defines when the work should start and the expected completion date.
- **Instruction to Bidders**: Outlines the rules for the bidding process, including how to ask questions and how the winner will be chosen.

## Why start with a formal ITB?

Informal bidding leads to confusion, cost overruns, and legal disputes. A formal ITB ensures that everyone is bidding on the same work with the same expectations.

- **Fair Competition**: Level playing field for all contractors.
- **Cost Control**: Standardized pricing makes comparison easy.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Clearly defines expectations before work starts.

## Common Questions

### What is the difference between an ITB and an RFP?

An ITB (Invitation to Bid) is typically used when the project requirements are very specific and the primary deciding factor is price. An RFP (Request for Proposal) is used when the project is complex and you want to see the contractor's proposed solution and qualifications in addition to price.

### What should be included in an Invitation to Bid?

A standard ITB should include a detailed scope of work, technical specifications, project timeline, bid submission deadline, required contractor qualifications, and the criteria that will be used to award the contract.

### How do I ensure a fair bidding process?

Provide the exact same information to all bidders at the same time. Use a standardized bid form (included in this template) so that you can compare costs 'apples-to-apples' across different contractors.

### Can I reject all bids?

Yes, most ITBs include a 'Reservation of Rights' clause that allows the project owner to reject any or all bids, or to waive informalities in the bids if it's in their best interest.
