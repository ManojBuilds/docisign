## What's inside this contract?

A comprehensive snow removal agreement that protects both you and your clients. Define exactly when you'll show up, what you'll clear, and how much it costs.

### Key Features

- **Trigger Depth Clause**: Specify the exact snowfall amount that activates service
- **Response Time Guarantee**: Define how quickly you'll arrive after snowfall
- **Seasonal vs Per-Event**: Flexible pricing models for different client needs
- **Liability Protection**: Clear language about property damage and injury claims

## Why snow removal businesses need contracts

A handshake deal won't hold up when a client refuses to pay because you plowed at 4am instead of 5am. This contract eliminates gray areas.

- **Lock in Seasonal Revenue**: Get paid upfront or in installments
- **Clear Expectations**: No arguments about when you should show up
- **Mobile Signing**: Close deals from your truck between routes

## Common Questions

### Should I use seasonal or per-event pricing?

Seasonal contracts provide predictable revenue and lock in clients for the whole winter. Per-event pricing works better for areas with unpredictable snowfall. Many providers offer both options.

### What's a good trigger depth?

Most commercial contracts use 2-3 inches as the trigger depth. Residential clients often prefer 3-4 inches to reduce frequency and cost.

### Am I liable if someone slips after I plow?

This contract includes clear timing language and liability limits. You're responsible for prompt service after snowfall, but not for ice formation or falls that occur between service visits. Always carry insurance.

### Can I charge extra for ice removal?

Yes! The template includes separate pricing for ice removal, extra salt application, and emergency callouts. Make sure these are clearly defined upfront.
