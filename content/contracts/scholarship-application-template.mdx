Simplify your scholarship selection process. This comprehensive template helps you collect consistent applicant data for fair and efficient evaluation.

## What's inside this scholarship template?

A multi-section application designed to capture a 360-degree view of every applicant.

### Key Features

- **Academic Achievement Section**: Fields for GPA, standardized test scores, and honors/awards.
- **Financial Need Assessment**: A dedicated area for applicants to explain their financial situation and upload supporting documents.
- **Personal Statement Prompt**: A customizable section for the applicant's essay, giving you insight into their character and goals.
- **Reference Contact List**: Built-in fields for teachers, mentors, or employers to serve as professional references.

## Why use a standardized application?

Vague applications lead to inconsistent data, making it impossible to compare students fairly. This template ensures you get the specific metrics you need to make an informed decision.

- **Efficiency**: Easy to read and score across multiple reviewers.
- **Completeness**: Captures academic, personal, and financial data.
- **Verification**: Includes a legal 'Truth and Accuracy' statement.

## Common Questions

### What information should I collect from scholarship applicants?

You should collect personal details, academic history (GPA and transcripts), extracurricular involvement, financial need information, and a personal statement or essay. Recommendation letters are also highly recommended.

### How do I ensure a fair scholarship selection process?

Use a standardized application like this one to ensure every applicant provides the same categories of information. Consider using a 'blind' review process where personal identifiers are removed before evaluation.

### Can I collect financial information securely?

Yes. When collecting sensitive financial data, ensure you are using a secure platform like BoopSign for e-signatures and document submission. Always store this data in a compliant, secure database.

### Should scholarship applications be legally binding?

The application itself is typically a request for information. However, the signature section (included) should verify that all information provided is true and accurate, allowing you to revoke aid if fraud is later discovered.
