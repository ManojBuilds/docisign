Quit gracefully. This professional resignation template helps you leave on good terms—critical for references, LinkedIn recommendations, and your professional reputation.

## What's in this resignation letter?

A respectful, professional template that hits all the right notes. Clear notice period, transition commitment, and gratitude without oversharing.

### Key Features

- **Proper Notice**: Clearly states your last day and notice period
- **Transition Language**: Shows you're committed to helping during the handoff
- **Professional Tone**: Gracious without being overly emotional or negative
- **Optional Reason**: Placeholder for explaining why you're leaving (if you want)

## Why your resignation letter matters

This isn't just formality—it's your last impression. A professional resignation letter protects your reputation, maintains relationships, and keeps doors open.

- **Protect Your Reputation**: This goes in your HR file permanently
- **Future References**: Your boss might be a reference someday
- **Industry Reputation**: Word travels fast in small industries

## Common Questions

### Do I need to explain why I'm leaving?

No. While you can include a brief reason if you want, you're not required to. Keep it simple and professional—moving to a new opportunity, pursuing other interests, or no reason at all.

### How much notice should I give?

Two weeks is standard in most industries. Check your employment contract for required notice periods. Senior roles often require 30+ days. Never quit without notice unless it's an emergency.

### Should I submit this via email or in person?

Best practice: tell your manager in person first, then follow up with the written letter via email. If remote, schedule a video call to deliver the news, then send the letter immediately after.

### What if I'm leaving on bad terms?

Stay professional anyway. This letter becomes part of your permanent HR file. Future employers will call for references. Don't burn bridges—write a neutral, gracious letter regardless of how you feel.
