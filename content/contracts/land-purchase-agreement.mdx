## What's inside this land purchase agreement?

A comprehensive contract that protects both buyer and seller. Covers all the unique aspects of land transactions that standard real estate contracts miss.

### Key Features

- **Survey Requirements**: Specifies who pays and what type of survey is needed
- **Zoning Contingencies**: Protects buyer if land can't be used as intended
- **Access & Easements**: Clarifies legal access to the property
- **Soil & Environmental Testing**: Contingencies for perc tests and contamination checks

## Why land deals need special contracts

Standard real estate contracts don't cover land-specific issues. Without proper contingencies, you could buy unusable land with no recourse.

- **Survey Protection**: Verify boundaries before closing
- **Zoning Safety**: Ensure land can be used as planned
- **Access Rights**: Confirm legal access to the property

## Common Questions

### What's different about buying land vs. a house?

Land purchases have unique challenges: no utilities, access issues, zoning restrictions, soil testing needs, and survey requirements. This agreement addresses all of these. You also typically need more earnest money (5-10% vs. 1-3% for homes).

### Do I need a survey for land?

Absolutely. Never skip the survey. It confirms boundaries, reveals encroachments, identifies easements, and verifies the actual acreage. Surveys cost $500-$3,000 but prevent $50,000+ disputes later.

### What if the land can't be built on?

That's why zoning and soil testing contingencies are critical. This agreement includes contingency periods for percolation tests, environmental studies, and zoning verification. If the land won't perc or can't be zoned for your use, you get your deposit back.

### How does land financing work?

Land loans are tougher to get and have higher rates (1-2% above home mortgages). Many land deals use owner financing. This template works for both scenarios and includes seller financing terms.
