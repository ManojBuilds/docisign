Formalize your business alliances. This agreement defines roles, responsibilities, and profit-sharing for two companies working toward a common goal.

## What's inside this partnership agreement?

A high-level business contract focused on growth, clarity, and protection.

### Key Features

- **Roles & Responsibilities Matrix**: A clear breakdown of exactly what each company is providing (financial resources, labor, tech, or branding).
- **IP & Confidentiality Protection**: Ensures that your proprietary trade secrets and pre-existing intellectual property remain yours.
- **Revenue Sharing Model**: Comprehensive formulas for how money will be collected, accounted for, and distributed between the partners.
- **Dispute Resolution Process**: Outlines how the partners will handle disagreements (mediation or arbitration) to avoid expensive litigation.

## Why formalize your alliances?

Handshake deals often fail when money or intellectual property is involved. A formal agreement ensures both partners are aligned on expectations and protected if things go wrong.

- **Alignment**: Ensures both companies are working toward the same KPIs.
- **Protection**: Safeguards your confidential data and brand.
- **Scalability**: Provides a clear roadmap for growing the shared venture.

## Common Questions

### What is a Strategic Partnership?

A strategic partnership is a formal relationship between two businesses to cooperate on a specific project or shared goal while remaining independent entities. It is less formal than a merger but more structured than a referral agreement.

### Who owns the Intellectual Property in a partnership?

The agreement includes an 'Intellectual Property' clause. Typically, each company retains ownership of any IP they had before the partnership. Any new IP created jointly is usually owned either by one party (with a license to the other) or jointly.

### How do we handle revenue and cost sharing?

You should include a dedicated 'Financial Arrangements' section. This should specify exactly who collects the money from clients and how and when it is split between the partners.

### What happens if one partner wants to leave?

The agreement provides a 'Term and Termination' section. This outlines the notice period required to end the partnership and how existing clients and data will be handled after the split (the 'Exit Strategy').
