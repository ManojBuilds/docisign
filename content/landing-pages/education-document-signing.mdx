## Why Traditional Document Signing Fails Educational Institutions

Most educational institutions still rely on outdated document handling methods that create unnecessary friction. When you send home a paper form for parents to sign and return, you're building barriers between communication and compliance. Even traditional e-signature tools often require parents to create accounts, remember passwords, or navigate complex interfaces.

These friction points don't just slow down your process—they can cause important documents to be delayed or lost entirely. In educational settings where timing is crucial (like enrollment deadlines or permission slips for field trips), these delays can disrupt carefully planned activities and programs.
