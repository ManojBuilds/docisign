The solution? A streamlined, digital document signing process that respects both HR professionals' and employees' time while maintaining the security and compliance standards required in HR settings.

## Why Traditional HR Document Signing Fails Organizations

Most HR departments still rely on outdated document handling methods that create unnecessary friction. When you require employees to print, sign, and return documents, you're building barriers between communication and compliance. Even traditional e-signature tools often require employees to create accounts, remember passwords, or navigate complex interfaces.

These friction points don't just slow down your process—they can cause delays in onboarding, benefits enrollment, and other critical HR functions. In today's fast-paced business environment where new hires expect seamless digital experiences, these delays can impact employee satisfaction and productivity.
