BoopSign removes this barrier entirely. Our "account-less" signing experience means your client can sign the moment they click the link, whether they are on their phone at a café or at their desk between meetings. By streamlining this final hurdle, you reclaim hours every month that can be reinvested into project work or much-needed rest.

This ensures your contracts are compliant with global standards like the ESIGN Act and UETA. Don't leave your business vulnerable to "I didn't see that clause." With BoopSign, you have a digital paper trail that protects your work and your livelihood.

## Common Freelance Documents You Can Sign

Stop printing and scanning. You can use BoopSign for every legal touchpoint in your freelance workflow.

### 1. Master Service Agreements (MSA)
The foundation of long-term relationships. Define the general terms of your partnership once, so you don't have to renegotiate legalities for every new project.

### 2. Statements of Work (SOW)
The specifics of a project. Detail your deliverables, timelines, and milestones here to keep scope creep at bay and ensure both parties are aligned.

### 3. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA)
Protecting confidentiality. Crucial for when you're working with sensitive client data, proprietary code, or pre-launch products.

### 4. Project Proposals
More than just a pitch. A signed proposal acts as an early commitment, ensuring both parties are aligned on the vision before the formal contract.

### 5. Payment Agreements
Get your money right. Specify deposit amounts, payment schedules (Net 15, Net 30), and late fees to ensure you get paid for your expertise on time.

### 6. Copyright Transfer Forms
Handing over the keys. Legally transfer ownership of your creative work or intellectual property once the final invoice has been settled.

### 7. Freelance Contracts
The all-in-one protector. Combining service terms and basic project info, this is the bread and butter of your freelance legal protection.

### 8. W-9 Forms
Tax season simplified. Securely collect and sign taxpayer identification forms so your clients can report payments correctly without the back-and-forth.

### 9. Client Onboarding Forms
Start strong. Collect the technical details, login credentials, and brand assets you need to kick off a project without 20 separate email threads.

### 10. Change Order Forms
When things shift. Document and get signatures for additions to the original scope to ensure you're fairly compensated for extra work.
