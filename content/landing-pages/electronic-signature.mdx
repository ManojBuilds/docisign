Know exactly what happened to your document. Our audit trails capture everything from the moment the document was sent to when it was downloaded by the final signatory. This includes IP addresses, timestamps, and browser information.

### 2. Custom Templates
Don't start from scratch every time. Upload your standard NDAs, service agreements, or proposals once, save them as templates, and send them to new clients in seconds.

### 3. Integrated Client Experience
Our interface is white-labeled and clean. Your clients see your professional document, not our heavy branding. This builds trust and ensures they feel secure during the signing process.
