## The Legal Foundation: ESIGN and UETA

In the United States, two primary laws establish the legal framework for electronic signatures: the **Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce (ESIGN) Act** and the **Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA)**.

These laws state a simple but powerful principle: **A signature, contract, or other record relating to a transaction may not be denied legal effect, validity, or enforceability solely because it is in electronic form.**

## Are There Any Documents I Can't Sign Electronically?

While 99% of business documents are perfectly suited for e-signatures, some jurisdictions still require "wet signatures" for specific high-stakes life events:
- Wills and Codicils
- Adoption or Divorce papers
- Court orders or certain official court documents
- Notices of default or foreclosure
