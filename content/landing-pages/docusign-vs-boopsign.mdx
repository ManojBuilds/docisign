## Why DocuSign Falls Short for Freelancers and Small Teams

DocuSign was built for enterprise use cases with complex requirements. While it's a powerful tool, it's often overkill for freelancers and small teams. The platform is laden with features that most solo professionals will never use, from bulk sending capabilities to complex approval workflows.

More importantly, DocuSign's design philosophy centers around the sender's needs rather than the signer's experience. This often results in a complex interface that creates friction for your clients, who may need to create accounts, verify identities multiple times, or navigate through multiple screens to complete a simple signature.
