## Why 2,847+ Businesses Switched to Boopsign

When you're comparing **Boopsign vs. DocuSign**, you're really looking at two different generations of software. DocuSign solved the "physical paper" problem of the early 2000s. Boopsign is solving the "digital friction" problem of today.

## The Friction Gap: Why Contracts Die in DocuSign

Research shows that **35% of people abandon an e-signature request** the moment they are asked to create an account or remember a password. 

Legacy tools like DocuSign often push signers to create accounts to fuel their own user growth. At Boopsign, we value *your* deal closure over *our* user count. By removing the login requirement, we ensure your contracts get signed the first time they are opened.

## Transparent Pricing vs. Hidden Limits

DocuSign’s pricing can be deceptive. Many users start on an "Individual" or "Standard" plan only to find they have a strict "Envelope" (document) limit per month or per year. 

**Boopsign offers a simple path:**
- **No Per-User Fees**: Add your team without doubling your bill.
- **No Document Limits**: Send 1 contract or 100; it's the same price.
- **Flat $20/month**: No enterprise upselling calls.

## Mobile Signing: The Desktop-First Problem

DocuSign was built for desktops. While they have an app, the "signing experience" for your average client—who receives your email on their phone—is often a clunky, shrunken-down version of a desktop site.

Boopsign is **mobile-native**. Our signing interface was designed specifically for thumbs and touchscreens. Your clients can sign with a single tap while standing in line for coffee, with zero frustration.
