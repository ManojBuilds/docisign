Traditional methods like email attachments, paper forms, and manual follow-ups create friction that can turn prospects into lost opportunities.

The solution? A streamlined, digital onboarding process that respects your client's time while collecting all the information you need to deliver exceptional service.

## Why Traditional Client Onboarding Fails Service Businesses

Most service businesses still rely on outdated onboarding methods that create unnecessary friction. When you email a PDF form and ask clients to print, sign, and scan it back, you're building barriers between interest and engagement. Even traditional e-signature tools often require clients to create accounts, remember passwords, or navigate complex interfaces.

These friction points don't just slow down your process—they can cause potential clients to reconsider working with you altogether. In today's fast-paced business environment, clients expect seamless digital experiences, especially for routine tasks like filling out intake forms.

While Boopsign accepts any PDF, consider creating templates that are optimized for mobile viewing. Large margins and clear section headers make it easier for clients to navigate and complete your forms quickly.

Boopsign provides real-time tracking so you know exactly when your client opens your onboarding documents. Use this intelligence to follow up at the right moment—when they're actively engaged with your process rather than wondering if they've seen it at all.
