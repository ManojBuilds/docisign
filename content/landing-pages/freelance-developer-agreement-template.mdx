## The Ultimate Guide to Freelance Developer Agreements in 2026

In software development, "simple" requests can turn into complex architecture changes over a weekend. If you are a freelance developer, your contract is your most important documentation—it defines the scope, the ownership, and the boundaries of your maintenance.

This guide explains how to use our **freelance developer agreement template** to stay protected while building great products.

## The 3 Technical Clauses Every Developer Needs

### 1. Acceptance Testing & Milestones
Never launch a site without an "Acceptance Signature." Our template breaks the project into milestones. After each milestone, the client has 5 days to test the code and report bugs. Once they sign off, that phase is legally "done," preventing them from asking for changes later for free.

### 2. The Bug-Fixing Window
Clients often expect a lifetime warranty on code. This is impossible given how fast browsers and APIs change. 

The **Boopsign Developer Template** includes a **30-Day Bug Warranty**. Within 30 days of launch, you fix true bugs (errors in your original code) for free. After 30 days, or for any new features, work is billed at your standard maintenance rate.

### 3. Background Intellectual Property
Many developers use their own "boilerplate" code or private libraries to speed up work. Without a specific IP clause, a client could technically claim they own your helper libraries too.

Our agreement distinguishes between **"Deliverables"** (which the client owns) and **"Developer IP"** (which you own but license to them). 

## Hosting, Domain & API Responsibilities

One of the biggest headaches for freelance developers is when a client's site goes down because they forgot to pay their hosting bill.

**Our template explicitly states:**
*   Client is responsible for all third-party fees (Hosting, API keys, Domain registration).
*   Developer is NOT liable for outages caused by hosting providers.
*   The Developer's role as a "Consultant" ends upon handover unless a separate **Maintenance Agreement** is signed.

## Frequently Asked Questions for Developers

### Who owns the GitHub repository?
Our template recommends that ownership of the code repository transfers to the client only after the final payment has been cleared.

### Does this cover security vulnerabilities?
The agreement includes a "Best Efforts" clause for security but limits your liability for zero-day exploits or third-party breaches outside of your control.

### Can I use this for White-Label work?
Yes. There is a specific section for "Non-Disclosure" that ensures you protect the end-client's identity while working as a subcontractor for another agency.
