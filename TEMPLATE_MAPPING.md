## Recommended Additional Templates: 17 Templates

### 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (7 Templates)
These templates cover the most common freelancer roles and have the highest demand:

1. **Service Agreement** 
   - For: Virtual Assistants, Business Coaches, OBMs
   - Why: Catch-all for service-based businesses
   
2. **Consulting Agreement**
   - For: Business Coaches, Consultants, SEO Specialists, Security Consultants
   - Why: Protects consultants from liability
   
3. **Photography/Videography Contract**
   - For: Wedding Videographers, Motion Graphics Artists
   - Why: High-value creative work needs strong IP protection
   
4. **Creative Services Agreement**
   - For: UI/UX Designers, Brand Designers, Illustrators, Interior Designers
   - Why: Covers all design disciplines
   
5. **Website Development Agreement**
   - For: Shopify Developers, No-Code Developers, Game Developers
   - Why: Technical scope and support needs clear definition
   
6. **Social Media Management Agreement**
   - For: Content Strategists, Copywriters, Influencer Marketing Managers
   - Why: Growing industry with unique content ownership issues
   
7. **Marketing Services Agreement**
   - For: SEO Specialists, Facebook Ads Managers, Google Ads Specialists, Email Marketers
   - Why: Protects against algorithm changes and performance expectations

### 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY (7 Templates)

8. **Event Planning Agreement**
   - For: Event Planners, Wedding Videographers
   - Why: Force majeure and vendor coordination issues
   
9. **Coaching Agreement**
   - For: Personal Trainers, Tutors, Business Coaches
   - Why: Results disclaimers and session policies
   
10. **Translation Services Agreement**
    - For: Translators, Technical Writers
    - Why: Liability for nuanced meanings
    
11. **Virtual Assistant Agreement**
    - For: Virtual Assistants, OBMs, Bookkeepers
    - Why: SLA and working hours boundaries
    
12. **Influencer Marketing Agreement**
    - For: Influencer Marketing Managers, Content Strategists
    - Why: FTC compliance and usage rights
    
13. **Audio/Video Production Agreement**

14. **Real Estate Services Agreement**
