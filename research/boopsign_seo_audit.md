## Critical Problems Identified

### 1. **ZERO Freelancer-Specific Optimization**
Your top-performing page is `/online-signature-maker` with 7 clicks, but I found only ONE page targeting freelancers: `/esignature-for-freelancers`. This is nowhere near enough.

### 2. **Weak Search Query Targeting**
Top query driving traffic: "Boopsign" (brand name)
- You're only ranking for your own brand
- No rankings for high-value freelancer keywords
- Missing opportunities in "electronic signature for freelancers," "freelance contract template," etc.

### 3. **Poor CTR Performance**
At 0.5% CTR, 99.5% of people who see your site in search results are NOT clicking. This indicates:
- Weak meta descriptions
- Non-compelling titles
- Lack of trust signals in SERPs

### 4. **Content Gaps**
Competitors like BoldSign, SignHouse, and Zoho Sign dominate freelancer searches because they have:
- Dedicated freelancer landing pages
- Freelancer-specific blog content
- Use case documentation
- Freelancer contract templates
- Freelancer pricing pages

### 5. **Low Domain Authority**
Your average position of 64.8 suggests:
- New or low domain authority
- Minimal backlinks
- Limited content depth
- Poor internal linking structure

**Problem:** Your sitemap might not be optimized

1. Ensure all important pages are in sitemap
2. Remove low-value pages
3. Add priority tags (freelancer pages = 0.9, others = 0.7)

### Issue 2: Site Speed
**Check:** Run PageSpeed Insights
**Common Issues:**
- Large images not optimized
- No lazy loading
- Render-blocking JavaScript

**Fixes:**
- Compress all images (use WebP format)
- Implement lazy loading
- Minify CSS/JS
- Use CDN

### Issue 3: Mobile Optimization
**Your USP is "Mobile-First"** - ensure it's actually fast on mobile
**Test:** Google Mobile-Friendly Test
**Common Issues:**
- Text too small
- Clickable elements too close
- Viewport not set
